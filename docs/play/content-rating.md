# Google Play Content Rating — Iraq Smart Maps

> IARC questionnaire answers for Play Console → App content → Content rating

## App category

**Maps & Navigation** (primary) / **Travel & Local** (secondary)

## Questionnaire answers

### Violence
- Does your app contain violence? **No**
- Does your app contain references to violence? **No**

### Sexuality
- Does your app contain sexual content or nudity? **No**

### Language
- Does your app contain strong language? **No**

### Controlled substances
- Does your app contain references to tobacco, alcohol, or drugs? **No**

### Gambling
- Does your app simulate gambling? **No**

### User-generated content (UGC)
- Does your app allow users to share content publicly? **Yes** (Discover feed: photos and comments from providers and users)
- Is there a content moderation system? **Yes** — reports trigger admin review; content can be hidden by moderators (M6)
- Is there profile sharing / direct messaging? **Yes** — messaging between users and providers (M4)
- Does the app share precise location? **Yes** — used for navigation, not shared with other users

### In-app purchases
- Does your app contain in-app purchases? **No**

### Ads
- Does your app contain ads? **No**

## Expected rating

Based on the above: **Everyone (E)** or **Everyone 10+** (due to UGC/messaging). No content that would trigger a higher rating.

## Notes for reviewer

- The app's primary language is Arabic (RTL). All moderation tools are functional (M6).
- The messaging system connects users to businesses; it is not a general social messaging platform.
- User photos are from providers (businesses) or from Discover feed; content policy prohibits inappropriate content.
