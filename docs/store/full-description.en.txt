Iraq Smart Maps — Your Intelligent Guide to Baghdad

Explore Baghdad with a full Arabic map powered by OpenStreetMap, plus an AI assistant that understands Iraqi dialect and helps you plan your day.

🗺️ MAP & SEARCH
• Interactive map in Arabic and Kurdish Sorani
• Search restaurants, hospitals, hotels, landmarks — with Iraqi spelling normalization
• One tap to center on your current location
• Optional satellite imagery layer

🧭 DIRECTIONS & NAVIGATION
• Car and walking routes with distance and ETA
• Turn-by-turn navigation with Arabic voice instructions
• Automatic rerouting when you go off-track
• Recent routes saved in My Activity

🤖 AI ASSISTANT (Arabic)
• Ask in colloquial Iraqi: "Where's a good restaurant near me?" or "Plan a day in Rusafa"
• Suggests 3–7 real, documented places with prices and crowd levels
• Plans a full day with a logical geographic route
• Contacts businesses on your behalf — only after your confirmation

💬 MESSAGING
• Send an inquiry to up to 5 places at once
• Receive business replies directly in the app
• WhatsApp handoff option for businesses that prefer it

🏪 PROVIDER (BUSINESS) MODE
• Register your business or claim its OSM listing
• Add photos, hours, prices, and social links
• Manage customer inquiries from your inbox
• Full control over which data is shown publicly

📸 DISCOVER BAGHDAD
• Browse photos from verified businesses and users
• Like, comment, and share posts
• Tap any post to open its location on the map

🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY
• We do not sell or share your data with advertisers
• Delete your account and all data anytime from Settings
• Location is only sent to the server when you search or navigate
